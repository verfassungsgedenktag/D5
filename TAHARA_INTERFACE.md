# Tahara Theorem 4.3.3: transcription audit

The formal statement is `D5/Tahara.lean`. It was checked against printed
pages 79--81 of `sources/нормальные ряды.pdf` (Theorem 4.3.3 appears on
printed pages 80--81).

## Context before the theorem

`D5.Tahara.Context` records:

- a finite group of nilpotency class at most four (`gamma G 5 = ⊥`);
- ordered cyclic bases for `G/γ₂`, `γ₂/γ₃`, and `γ₃/γ₄`;
- their positive orders `d`, `e`, and `f`, with the divisibility chains;
- structural coefficients `b`, `c`, `delta`, and `alpha`;
- the three structural relations preceding Theorem 4.3.3, modulo `γ₄`.

`D5.Tahara.IsCyclicBasis` is not an additional axiom. It defines “cyclic
basis” as existence and uniqueness of the ordered normal form with exponents
in the ranges `0, ..., order(i)-1`.

## The displayed word

`D5.Tahara.word` is formula (4.3.1). All products use `orderedProduct` and
`orderedProductWhere`, based on `List.finRange`, so they retain the source's
lexicographic order in the noncommutative group.

## Conditions

The integer parameters are stored in `D5.Tahara.Parameters`. The declarations
`Condition2` through `Condition15` correspond one-for-one to equations
(4.3.2) through (4.3.15). `Parameters.Satisfies` is their conjunction.

Congruences use `Int.ModEq`. A modulus written `mod(d(i), e(k))` in the source
is represented by `gcd(d(i), e(k))`.

## Printed index slips

Three bounds in the printed display conflict with the parameter declarations
immediately above them. The formalization uses the type-correct readings,
which are also used in the subsequent proof and in `proof_D5_G_gamma6.md`:

1. The first product of (4.3.1) uses `i < j`, since `u_ij` is defined only
   for `i < j`.
2. Condition (4.3.10) uses `k ≤ s`, since `w_ijk`, `w'_ijk`, and `w''_ijk`
   are indexed by the first cyclic basis. The printed `k ≤ t` is a typo.
3. Condition (4.3.13) uses the third-basis index `l ≤ r` (denoted `u` in the
   cited source). The printed `l ≤ s` is a typo.

## Axiom boundary

`D5.Tahara.description` is the only axiom in this file. It states that, for a
finite group with a `Context`, `D₅(G)` equals the subgroup generated by the
words (4.3.1) whose parameters satisfy (4.3.2)--(4.3.15).

The derived theorems `mem_dimensionSubgroup_five_iff` and
`word_mem_dimensionSubgroup_five` only rewrite or apply this equality. The
axiom audit confirms that they introduce no further mathematical assumption.
